## Quantum mechanics
Start from the five postulate, we construct a new framework for the microscopic particles, including single particle quantization, multiparticles second quantization, the stationary solution, scattering theory and perturbation theory

## Statistical mechanics
We build a framework for studying equilibrium of the large number system. we introduce the framework of statistical ensamble, and introduce the classical boltzmann statistic, quantum fermi-dirac statistic and boson-einstein statistics. Furthermore, we introduce the non-equilibrium statistic and boltzmann equation.
